How to play

Click a blue unit → blue tiles show its movement range (cardinal steps only). Click a tile to move. Enemies in range glow red — click one to attack (hover for the battle forecast). Click anywhere else to wait. Healers highlight wounded allies in green.

Battle timing: when the ring shrinks onto the target, press Space (or click) at the moment they align. Each class has its own attack rhythm — chain every press perfectly for MAX! damage (1.5×). Blue = physical attack, green = magic, red = brace to halve an incoming hit.

Simulate Battle lets the AI play both armies to the end (no QTEs — the AI fights alone).
