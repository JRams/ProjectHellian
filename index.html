turn-based tactics demo — Grimwater Crossing
Turn 1 — Player Phase

How to play

Click a blue unit → blue tiles show its movement range (cardinal steps only). Click a tile to move. Enemies in range glow red — click one to attack (hover for the battle forecast). Click anywhere else to wait. Healers highlight wounded allies in green.

Simulate Battle lets the AI play both armies to the end.
